## Why the numbers differ (specific mechanism)

Each round trip trades about $201,953 of notional
(entry and exit, both legs), so the 5 bps of half-spread plus slippage costs about
$100.98 per trade. Commissions add
$8.77 and short borrow $17.17, for an
average of **$126.92 per trade**. That compares with an average
gross P&L of **+$162.67 per trade**, which is
8.1 bps of the notional each
trade turns over. Over 20 trades (8.1× capital traded
per year), costs total $2,538.32, equal to
**78% of the gross P&L**. Net P&L would reach zero at
**1.28× the base-case costs**.
